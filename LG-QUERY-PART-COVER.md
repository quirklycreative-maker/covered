## What the owner's manuals already say (read 25 Oct 2026)

- The top-load (T70VBMB1Z, T80VBMB4Z) and semi-auto (P7020NGAZ, P1155SKAZ) manuals print a warranty: 2 years on all parts except plastic parts, consumables, filters, knobs, hoses and top cover, plus an extra motor-only cover (top-load inverter motor 8 more years; semi-auto wash motor 3 more years, service charge payable). That supports the 2-year warranty governing parts, but it does not mention the 6- and 12-month Part Warranty Period table and is not a written answer to the three questions.
- The front-load manuals (FHB1207Z2M/Z2W, FHP1209Z5M) carry no warranty text; they refer to the warranty card. So the reply is most needed for front-load, and question 2 (failed front-load PCB in month 14) is the one the manuals cannot answer.
- You may mention the model's manual in the query if useful; links are in `lg-manuals.yaml`.

## Done when

A written LG reply, or warranty-card text, names the scope. Paste it back and the packs and `NOTES.md` get updated.

If there is no reply in 10 working days, ship with the cautious reading: the 2-year warranty governs, the part list applies only to repaired parts, and the claim template says "confirm with service centre" (हिंदी: "सर्विस सेंटर से पुष्टि करें").
